# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want pii-safe to be a welcoming project for thoughtful, respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially when reviewing edge cases or false positives.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and do not share real personal data in issues, tests, or examples.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person’s private information.
- Posting real secrets, tokens, credentials, or personal data.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct.

If you see a problem, please contact the maintainers privately.
